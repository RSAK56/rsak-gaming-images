Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

RSAK Gaming Uploader is an internal tool used only by RSAK Gaming to publish its own content to its own YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram accounts.

YouTube API Services

RSAK Gaming Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth, scope youtube.upload), the tool uploads videos to the RSAK Gaming YouTube channel. It does not read, collect, or access any other user's account or YouTube data.

What we collect, store, and share

We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data from viewers or other users. The only stored item is the channel owner's OAuth refresh token, kept as an encrypted secret and used only to upload RSAK Gaming's own videos. No cookies or tracking are used by the tool.

Data deletion and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page. When access is revoked or on request by email, the stored token is deleted within 7 days. Because no other user data is stored, there is nothing else to delete.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
