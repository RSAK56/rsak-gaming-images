Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

RSAK Gaming Uploader is an internal tool used only by RSAK Gaming to publish its own content to its own YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram accounts.

What we access

The tool uses Google OAuth with the YouTube upload permission, granted by the RSAK Gaming channel owner, to upload videos to the RSAK Gaming channel. It does not access any other user's account or data.

What we collect and share

We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data from viewers or other users. Access tokens are kept as encrypted secrets and used only to upload RSAK Gaming's own videos.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube content is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
