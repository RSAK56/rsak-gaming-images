Overview.

Behind the scenes

The RSAK Publishing Engine.

Our own automation turns the day's gaming headlines into polished posts for every platform, while a person stays in control of what goes out.

  1. 1

    Collect

    News feeds and alerts are gathered every morning into one queue, with duplicates removed.

  2. 2

    Curate

    Only gaming stories pass. Off-topic items, repeated stories and weak images are filtered out automatically.

  3. 3

    Create

    AI writes a short original caption. The engine builds a branded card from the story's lead image, plus vertical and square videos with royalty-free music.

    CaptionsBranded cardsMusic videos
  4. 4

    Approve

    Each draft lands in a review queue. Nothing is published until the channel owner approves it.

  5. 5

    Publish

    One approval publishes everywhere at once.
